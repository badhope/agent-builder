### Supported Formats
- Documents: PDF, DOCX, DOC, ODT, RTF, EPUB, MOBI
- Spreadsheets: XLSX, XLS, CSV, TSV, ODS
- Presentations: PPTX, PPT, ODP
- Markup: Markdown, HTML, TXT
- Data: JSON, XML, YAML
- Images: PNG, JPG, WEBP, BMP, TIFF, SVG
